LANDLORD PROPERTY INSURANCE POLICY SCHEDULE
Policy No: SC-LP-DM-2026-1182
Insurer: SafeCover Malaysia Berhad
Named Insured: Landlord One Sdn Bhd
Property: Damai Residence, 1 Jalan Damai, Keramat, W.P. Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Scope: Blanket landlord policy covering all landlord-owned units at Damai Residence (property-wide)
Policy Period: 01 January 2026 to 31 December 2026

SECTION A - COVERAGE LIMITS
A1. Property damage coverage limit: RM 120,000 per occurrence.
A2. Public liability coverage limit: RM 500,000 per occurrence.
A3. Alternative accommodation (tenant relocation): RM 6,000 aggregate limit.

SECTION B - DEDUCTIBLE / EXCESS
B1. Property damage deductible: RM 1,500 per incident.
B2. Water ingress deductible: RM 2,000 per incident.
B3. Malicious damage deductible: RM 1,000 per incident.

SECTION C - COVERED EVENTS
C1. Fire, lightning, and explosion.
C2. Burst pipe and sudden accidental water discharge.
C3. Riot and strike damage (subject to endorsement).
C4. Accidental damage to fixed building fixtures.

SECTION D - CLAIM CONDITIONS
D1. Claim must be reported within 7 calendar days from incident date.
D2. Supporting evidence includes:
- Incident photos and damage inventory
- Police report where applicable
- Repair quotations and contractor scope
D3. Insurer may appoint a loss adjuster before final claim approval.

SECTION E - NON-COVERED EVENTS
E1. Intentional damage by insured party.
E2. Normal wear and tear, corrosion, and gradual deterioration.
E3. Unreported long-term leaks and pre-existing defects.
E4. Vacancy beyond 60 consecutive days unless endorsed.

SECTION F - CLAIM PAYOUT BASIS
F1. Settlement is subject to deductible and policy limits.
F2. Betterment and depreciation adjustments may apply to old components.
F3. This policy applies to all covered units at the property and is not tied to any single unit.
