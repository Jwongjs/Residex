UTILITY BILLING TERMS AND TARIFF SUMMARY
Property: Damai Residence, Unit B-08-11, 1 Jalan Damai, Keramat, W.P. Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Account Reference: DM-UB0811-UTIL-2026

1. ELECTRICITY TARIFF (DOMESTIC)
1.1 Electricity usage is billed by meter consumption (kWh).
1.2 Standard rate for Unit B-08-11 is RM 0.55 per kWh.
1.3 Common-area and sinking-fund electricity charges are excluded from tenant utility billing.

2. WATER TARIFF
2.1 Water usage is billed by volume (cubic meter).
2.2 Water rate is RM 2.10 per cubic meter.
2.3 Sewer and environmental surcharges, where applicable, are listed as separate invoice lines.

3. METER READING AND BILLING CYCLE
3.1 Meter reading date is on or around the 28th of each month.
3.2 Billing period runs from the 1st to the last day of each month.
3.3 Invoice is issued within 3 business days after meter reading.
3.4 Payment due date is 14 days from invoice date.

4. PAYMENT AND PENALTY
4.1 Late payment charge is 1.0 percent of outstanding amount after due date.
4.2 Reconnection charges apply if supply is suspended due to non-payment.

5. RESPONSIBILITY MATRIX
5.1 Tenant pays variable usage charges for electricity and water.
5.2 Landlord pays fixed municipal connection fees and annual account maintenance fees.
5.3 Billing disputes must be raised in writing within 7 days of invoice issue date.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
6.1 On dispute, tenant may request meter re-check and consumption breakdown.
6.2 If overbilling is confirmed, adjustment is credited in the next billing cycle.
